Privacy policy

Personal backup is a single-user application used only by its owner.

  • It requests only the drive.file scope: access to files and folders it creates itself. It cannot see any other files in Google Drive.
  • It stores encrypted backup data in the owner's Google Drive and reads it back for restore. Data is encrypted before upload.
  • No data is collected, shared, sold or transferred to any third party. There are no analytics and no servers other than Google Drive.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
